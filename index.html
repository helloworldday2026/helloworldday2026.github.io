Hello World Day

Every year on November 30 · Monday, November 30, 2026

What is it?

A friendly yearly holiday for the AI age. Say hello to your AI, then give your devices a day off from demands. It's a fun community celebration, like Pi Day. Nobody has to switch anything off.

How to celebrate

  • Say "Hello" to your AI assistant, and mean it.
  • Give your devices a break: no demands, no doomscrolling.
  • Run your first "Hello, World!" in any language you like.
  • Set up something local: a voice assistant, a home server, an open source tool.
  • Share what you did with #HelloWorldDay

Why?

"Hello, World!" is the first program every coder writes. ELIZA, the 1966 chatbot, was among the first programs to talk back. And on November 30, 2022, ChatGPT launched to the public. This day celebrates that conversation, and the people freeing their gadgets from the corporate cloud with local, open and self-hosted tools.
